## What it is

- AI consulting-training platform for Wilfrid Laurier's StartUp Lab
- Professor writes a short prompt → system generates company backstory, org chart of AI employee personas, grading rubric
- Students interview the personas (text + ElevenLabs voice) in a Slack-style inbox, dig for a hidden core problem, submit deliverables, get AI stakeholder feedback + rubric grades

## The design idea

- Consulting skill = knowing what to ask; personas deliberately withhold from vague questions and open up when the student shows business understanding
- Personas have designed tension (curt exec who hates fishing expeditions vs. junior who overshares)
- Every student gets a private snapshot of the company at session start — template/session split carries the product
