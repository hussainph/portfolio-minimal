## What is it?

The Consulting Sandbox was an app designed to "invert" the traditional business case study by simulating stakeholder environments within a business using AI agents. In simpler terms, instead of reading a business case, you talk to simulated individuals associated with the business to figure out exactly what's wrong with the company. This way, you exercise the ability to think critically in live conversation while still exercising the business student in you.

## Why build it?

My team at the Lab was put in charge of creating a new Consulting program for undergrad business students. However, we recognized in our early pilots that younger students struggled to manage meetings with stakeholders; they were often quiet and struggled to engage stakeholders, which left companies feeling a little confused as to whether they got the message across. Of course, we couldn't train students ourselves as a team of 2.5-ish people, so I decided to pull out the engineer in me to build this app as a way to close the training gap.
