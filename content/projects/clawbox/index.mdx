## What it is

Clawbox was the precursor to the Grok bots and Hermes Agents of today; it allowed AI agents to share a unified workspace where they could work together on knowledge work problems. 

## Why build it?

I built Clawbox because I quickly noticed three huge problems with the OpenClaw trend:

1. Security
2. Setup complexity
3. Cost management

I figured that a shared cloud workspace where all the agents just "work" without needing to worry about setup or security was the natural progression, especially since the setup part was incredibly tricky even beyond the "get a Mac Mini" or "open a Hetzner Cloud instance" of it all.

## Status
- Clawbox was a passion project of mine but I recognized that it was a pretty expensive product and well-suited to enterprise teams.
- I also found that "do anything with agents" was a less comfortable niche than I could justify working in. I eventually took all of my learnings and pivoted towards Volli v2, which niched multi-agent systems specifically towards code.
